# Chro.mA


Chro.mA is an AI-powered platform that helps you explore the emotional landscape of your music and supports your mental wellness journey. By combining advanced machine learning, music psychology, and seamless Spotify integration, Chro.mA offers a unique way to understand yourself through the songs you love.

Chro.mA is more than just a music app. It is a wellness companion that analyzes your musical preferences, playlists, and listening history to provide:
- **Mood Analysis:** Discover the emotional tone of your favorite songs and playlists.
- **Personalized Recommendations:** Get song suggestions that match or uplift your current mood.
- **Mental Wellness Insights:** Use music as a tool for self-reflection and emotional growth.


## Our Philosophy
- **Music as a Mirror:** We believe music reflects our inner world. By understanding our musical choices, we can gain deeper insights into our emotions and mental state.
- **Wellness-Centered Design:** Every feature is built with mental health in mind, supporting your journey toward greater self-awareness and well-being.
- **Privacy First:** Your data is never stored permanently. All analysis is ephemeral and used only to empower you.
- **Science-Backed:** Our approach is grounded in music psychology research and evidence-based mental health practices.

## Key Features
- **AI-Powered Mood Analysis:** Advanced algorithms analyze your music to reveal emotional patterns.
- **Spotify Integration:** Connect your Spotify account to analyze playlists, history, and create new mood-based playlists.
- **Personal Growth:** Use insights from your music to support your mental wellness and discover new aspects of yourself.
- **Session-Based Experience:** All mood analysis is session-based and temporary, ensuring privacy and control.

## Technologies Used
- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion
- **Backend:** FastAPI, Python, MongoDB, Google Gemini, SerpAPI, Spotify API


## Get Started
1. Clone the repository
2. Follow setup instructions in the `frontend/` and `backend/` folders
3. Start your journey of musical self-discovery!
